Before You Ask

Frequently asked questions

How do I get a refund on a booking?

Contact us by phone or email with your booking code at least 2 hours before showtime, and our team will help process a refund or exchange.

Do your cinemas have wheelchair access?

Yes — all three locations are wheelchair accessible. If you need a specific seating arrangement, mention it when you contact us and we'll have it ready.

Can I order food ahead of my showtime?

Yes, use the Food & Drinks step during checkout, or order separately from our Concessions page once you have a booking.

How do I switch the site to Khmer?

Use the EN / KH toggle in the top navigation on the homepage. Your language choice is remembered on this device.
