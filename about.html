Our Story

Built for the love of the big screen

The Spoiler House is a Cambodian cinema chain bringing premium screens, real Khmer hospitality, and great food to Phnom Penh — one showtime at a time.

Who We Are

A hometown cinema, built for everyone

The Spoiler House started with a simple idea: Phnom Penh deserved a cinema that felt as good as the movies it showed. We built our first screen with that in mind, and every location since has followed the same standard — comfortable seating, sharp picture and sound, and a menu worth showing up early for.

Today we operate across three locations in Phnom Penh, each shaped by its neighborhood but sharing the same commitment to accessibility, comfort, and honest pricing. Whether you're catching a Khmer-language premiere, a subtitled Hollywood blockbuster, or bringing the whole family for a weekend matinee, we want every seat in the house to feel like a good one.

What Drives Us

Our values

  • 🎬

    Craft over gimmicks

    We invest in picture quality, sound, and seating first — the parts of the experience you actually feel, every single visit.

  • 🤝

    Access for everyone

    Bilingual EN/KH screens, wheelchair-accessible auditoriums, and assisted listening at select locations, so more people can enjoy the show.

  • 🍿

    Honest hospitality

    Clear pricing, fast concession lines, and a team that treats every guest — regular or first-timer — like they belong here.

Our Journey

How we got here

  1. Year 1

    Our first screen opens

    The Spoiler House opens its first location in Phnom Penh with a focus on comfort and sound quality.

  2. Year 2

    Concessions get a rebuild

    We rework our food and drink menu around fast service and combos guests actually order.

  3. Year 3

    A second and third location

    Two more cinemas open across the city, each adding new formats like IMAX and Diamond Class.

  4. Today

    Bilingual, online, and growing

    Booking, tickets, and the whole site are now available in English and Khmer, with more on the way.
